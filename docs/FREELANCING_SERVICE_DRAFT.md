# Invoice Automation Service — Draft

*Positioning draft for review. This is not a record of prior client work or a fixed service commitment.*

## Headline

**Turn repetitive spreadsheet-to-invoice work into a workflow tailored to your business.**

I can help small businesses assess and automate recurring invoice preparation from Excel or CSV data, including validation, branded PDF output, and batch processing where the requirements fit.

## Problems this service can address

- Re-entering spreadsheet data into the same invoice layout.
- Spending time creating, naming, and filing invoices one at a time.
- Finding inconsistent or missing customer, date, quantity, or amount values late in the process.
- Needing an invoice PDF layout that reflects an existing brand and approved billing process.

The right solution depends on the client's real files, volume, systems, and review requirements. Automation should not silently guess when source data is ambiguous.

## Possible deliverables

After requirements and scope are agreed, a project may include:

- A documented review of a representative, sanitized input file and current invoice process.
- CSV or Excel ingestion and field mapping for agreed columns and edge cases.
- Validation messages for missing, malformed, or conflicting values.
- A PDF template based on client-provided, approved branding and layout requirements.
- Single and batch PDF generation with agreed file-naming and output-folder behavior.
- Automated tests using synthetic or sanitized fixtures.
- A handoff guide and, if included in scope, a Windows package and installation walkthrough.

The final deliverables, source handoff, deployment method, and support terms must be written into the project agreement.

## Information needed before estimating

Please be ready to discuss:

- A sanitized sample of the input workbook/CSV and a representative desired invoice PDF.
- Which columns are required, optional, or allowed to be blank, including real examples of unusual rows.
- Expected invoice volume, batch size, and how often the workflow runs.
- Naming, numbering, storage, duplicate-file, and correction requirements.
- Branding files and approved content for the invoice template.
- The client's chosen calculation, tax, currency, rounding, and payment rules, confirmed by their accountant or other qualified adviser where appropriate.
- Target Windows versions, existing software integrations, deployment restrictions, and who will use the tool.
- Data sensitivity, permitted storage locations, retention expectations, and an agreed method for sharing any necessary sample data.
- Acceptance criteria, target schedule, handoff expectations, and support needs.

Do not send real customer data or credentials before a secure transfer and data-handling process has been agreed. A sanitized sample is usually sufficient for initial scoping.

## A realistic project workflow

1. **Discovery:** Walk through the existing process, inputs, exceptions, and desired outputs.
2. **Scope:** Agree on supported formats, rules, template, tests, deployment, acceptance criteria, and exclusions.
3. **Prototype:** Build a small workflow against synthetic or sanitized examples and review it with the client.
4. **Validation:** Test normal and edge cases, compare sample totals and PDFs against client-approved expectations, and fix agreed defects.
5. **Handoff:** Deliver the agreed code or package, usage notes, and a walkthrough. Confirm who owns ongoing operation and data backups.
6. **Support:** Provide only the support period and changes explicitly agreed for the project.

## Boundaries and assumptions

- The client is responsible for approving invoice content and supplying authoritative calculation and tax rules. This service does not provide accounting, tax, legal, or regulatory advice and does not promise compliance.
- Integrations, unusual spreadsheets, multiple jurisdictions, migration of old invoices, cloud hosting, email delivery, payment processing, and ongoing maintenance require separate scoping.
- Email server acceptance does not prove delivery or reading. Any external service depends on the client's accounts, access, and provider policies.
- Data storage, retention, access controls, backup, and deletion responsibilities must be agreed before handling production information.
- No delivery date, price, result, or ongoing support guarantee is implied by this draft; those depend on a written scope.
